How to Use This Dashboard

Move from broad market patterns to vehicle-level comparisons using the published Tableau views.

01 / EXPLORE

Filter the vehicle landscape

Start with manufacturer, model year, vehicle category, manufacturing country or market region.

02 / COMPARE

Balance specifications and cost

Compare driving range, battery capacity, charging metrics, price, insurance, warranty and resale estimates.

03 / INTERPRET

Consider context and trade-offs

Use sales, ratings, safety and estimated CO₂ savings as complementary indicators, not as a universal ranking.
